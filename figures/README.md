# Figures

All figures were produced by the main script ([src/20260818 Bundibugyo 2007 outbreak.py](../src/20260818%20Bundibugyo%202007%20outbreak.py)) from the posterior draws of the baseline model, unless stated otherwise. Each figure is provided as the PDF written by the script and as a PNG rendered from it at 300 dpi. The captions below are those of the published version of the paper.

| File | In the paper | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `forest_box_Bundibugyo` ([pdf](forest_box_Bundibugyo.pdf), [png](forest_box_Bundibugyo.png)) | Figure (main text) | Estimates of epidemiologic parameters for Bundibugyo virus from study of transmissibility and case fatality of Bundibugyo virus estimated from first outbreak, Uganda, 2007. Estimates are stratified by preintervention and postintervention periods. Each boxplot displays the interquartile range (box tops and bottoms), 95% CrI (whiskers), median (vertical line), and mean (solid circle). CFR, case-fatality rate; CrI, credible interval. |
| `R_CFR_ascertainment_Bundibugyo` ([pdf](R_CFR_ascertainment_Bundibugyo.pdf), [png](R_CFR_ascertainment_Bundibugyo.png)) | Appendix Figure 1 | Model fit to the data from the first Bundibugyo outbreak in Uganda, 2007. (A) Effective reproduction number (R<sub>eff</sub>) with the reported weekly incidence of fatal and non-fatal cases (legend) shown in the background. (B) Case-fatality rate (CFR) with weekly observed CFR estimates shown in the background; gray dots represent the posterior means from a β-binomial model with Jeffreys' prior, and whiskers represent the corresponding 95% CrIs. (C) Under-ascertainment of cases during the outbreak. In all panels, solid lines represent posterior means of the main model; light and dark shaded areas indicate 95% and 50% (interquartile) CrIs; the vertical dashed line represents the week of control measures implementation. |

R<sub>eff</sub>, effective reproduction number; CFR, case-fatality rate. |

Notes

* Appendix Figures 2 and 3 compare the baseline (step) model with the three fits of the ramp model (τ<sub>rep</sub> = 2, 4 and 8 weeks); Appendix Figure 4 uses the 100 fits of the partial-ascertainment grid. See [src/README.md](../src/README.md) for the models.
* In the published versions of Appendix Figures 1, 2, 4 and 5, the reproduction number is labeled R<sub>t</sub> instead of R<sub>eff</sub>; that change was made at the proof stage by editing the PDFs, and the files here are the unedited output of the script.
